About

About the band

City of Bristol Pipes and Drums is a volunteer band established in 1999 and based in Bristol, performing across the South West at civic, community and private events.

Background

The band grew from local piping activity in Bristol and developed into the city's own Highland pipe band. Since then it has become a regular presence at remembrance ceremonies, public parades, festivals and private occasions.

Players come from Bristol and the wider South West, combining an established performing core with newer members working toward public appearances.

At a glance

  • Established in 1999
  • Volunteer-led and self-funded
  • Weekly rehearsals in central Bristol
  • Public, civic and private performances

How the band works

Members are unpaid for performances. Fees from bookings go back into the practical costs that keep the band active, including instruments, maintenance, rehearsal space, travel and uniform.

The band aims to balance a friendly rehearsal culture with the discipline needed for public performance, especially at civic and remembrance events.

Today

City of Bristol Pipes and Drums continues to perform throughout the region and to welcome both experienced players and committed beginners looking for a route into pipe band performance.
