FAQ

Frequently asked questions

A few practical answers about bookings, rehearsals and joining the band.

Can we ask the band to play at our event?

Yes. The best first step is the booking page, which helps direct your enquiry to the right person.

How much does a booking cost?

Cost depends on the type of event, location, duration and whether you need the full band, a smaller ensemble or an individual player.

Where does the band rehearse?

The band rehearses weekly in central Bristol.

Can beginners join?

Yes, but progress takes commitment, regular practice and time. The band can help point enthusiastic beginners toward tuition and the right next steps.

Can an experienced drummer join the snare line straight away?

Not always. Pipe band drumming has its own technique and repertoire, so some players may need time before taking a regular place in the performing set.

Still need help?

Use the booking page for event enquiries and the joining page for membership questions. General contact details are also available.
