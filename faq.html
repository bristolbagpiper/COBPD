Frequently asked questions

Open a question to see the answer!

We’ve seen you at an event, can you come and play for us?

We’d be happy to consider it, please contact us using the details on our ’Book us’ page and we can talk more.

How much do you charge for an event?

This price depends on a number of factors, with consideration given to variables such as the event size, host and size of band available.

How often, and where do you practice?

We practice together as a band, weekly within the centre of Bristol.

Can I join the band even if I have no experience of playing?

You don’t need to have played pipes or drums before. However, reaching the level to play within the band takes a large amount of practice and dedication and we ask musicians to commit to regular practices independently, as well as with us. We are happy to put you in touch with a tutor to help support enthusiastic and budding musicians.

I have experience of playing the drums - can I join your drum corps e.g. to play the snare?

It takes considerable time to become proficient in playing the snare. If not proficient already, we can put you in touch with a tutor to learn the basics, before you are able to join the band and learn our music.

Learners often have to learn to play our set before joining our line up.

Once competent this may involve playing the tenor drum for a period and subbing in to play snare when space becomes available.

Which method is best to contact your band?

Please try completing one of our contact forms first. This will help direct your email to the right person quicker. If you don’t have a response in a reasonable time frame, please try again or reach out to us by email or via our social media pages - Instagram and Facebook. We all have busy day jobs, and may just need a gentle nudge.
