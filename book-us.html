Book us

Booking information

The band is available for civic appearances, remembrance events, public parades, festivals, weddings, memorials and private ceremonial occasions.

What the band can provide

Depending on the occasion, the band can discuss a full parade presence, a smaller ensemble or individual players.

  • Civic ceremonies and remembrance services
  • Street processions, carnivals and festivals
  • Weddings, anniversaries and birthdays
  • Funerals, memorials and smaller ceremonial performances

Information to include

When getting in touch, it helps to include the date, location, type of event and the sort of line-up you have in mind. If you are not sure what is possible, the band can discuss options with you.

  • Date and approximate timing
  • Location and travel details
  • Type of event
  • Whether you need the full band, a smaller group or an individual player

Booking details

Cost depends on the type of event, location, duration and size of the required line-up. Fees support the practical running costs of the band rather than individual performers.
