Book us

Booking information

The band is available for civic appearances, remembrance events, public parades, festivals, weddings, memorials and private ceremonial occasions.

Format Full band for processions and formal appearances
Format Smaller ensemble when a lighter footprint is needed
Format Individual players for quieter ceremonial moments

What the band can provide

Depending on the occasion, the band can discuss a full parade presence, a smaller ensemble or individual players.

  • Civic ceremonies and remembrance services
  • Street processions, carnivals and festivals
  • Weddings, anniversaries and birthdays
  • Funerals, memorials and smaller ceremonial performances

Booking notes

Cost depends on the event, location, duration and size of the required line-up.

Fees support the practical running costs of the band rather than individual performers.

How to enquire

The quickest route is the live booking form on the current band site. Email is also available if you already know what you need.
