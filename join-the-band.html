Join the band

Joining information

The band welcomes experienced pipers and drummers, and beginners who are prepared to work steadily toward public performance.

Who can join

Players come from Bristol and the wider South West. If you already play, you may be able to move into rehearsals quickly. If you are starting from scratch, the path is longer, but the band can help point you toward tuition and the right next steps.

  • Pipers
  • Snare drummers
  • Tenor and bass drummers

For beginners

Beginners are welcome, but pipe band performance takes commitment. Pipe band drumming has its own technique and style, so players with general drumming experience may still need time and tuition before joining the regular line-up.

What to expect

The band rehearses weekly in central Bristol. Joining usually involves regular individual practice, time to learn repertoire and a gradual route into the performing set.
