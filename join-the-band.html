Interested in joining the band?

An open invitation to new and existing musicians.

The City of Bristol Pipes and Drums extends a warm invitation to individuals who are interested in joining our esteemed pipe band. If you are an existing musician and desire to be a part of an exciting and dynamic group, we will be pleased to welcome you.
