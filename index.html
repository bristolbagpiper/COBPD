Bristol's volunteer pipe band.

Established in 1999, the band performs at civic ceremonies, remembrance events, festivals, weddings, memorials and parades across Bristol, the South West and beyond.

About the band

City of Bristol Pipes and Drums has become a familiar part of civic and community life in the city, appearing at remembrance ceremonies, festivals, carnivals and private occasions.

Members are unpaid for performances. Income from bookings supports instruments, uniform, maintenance, rehearsal space and travel.
