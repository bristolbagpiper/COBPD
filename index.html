Founded in 1999, the band performs at civic ceremonies, remembrance events, festivals, parades, weddings, memorials and other private occasions across Bristol and the South West.

About the band

City of Bristol Pipes and Drums is a volunteer band with charitable aims. Members are unpaid for performances, and income from bookings supports instruments, maintenance, rehearsal space, travel and uniform.

The band has become a regular part of civic and community life in Bristol, appearing at remembrance ceremonies, public events and private functions.
