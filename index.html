About the band

A volunteer pipe band representing Bristol at civic and public events.

A self-funded volunteer band with charitable objectives, our members receive no remuneration for band performances and, as the City of Bristol’s adopted pipe band, perform without charge at civic events in and around the city including the Annual Remembrance Day Parade, one of the biggest in the country.

The band takes bookings to play at festivals, street parades and other events, home and abroad, and fees charged for these performances go towards maintenance including: equipment, instruments, rehearsal-hall rent, uniforms and travel costs.

Membership

Open to all, with room for experienced players and beginners.

The band is open to all, and while some of us have Scottish roots, more than half of our members are from the South West. Experienced Pipers and Drummers studying or working in the area will find a ready welcome.

Beginners in piping or drumming are also very welcome. We will help with instruction but we expect considerable application and you will need to be able to travel independently (or with a parent / guardian) to the practice sessions which are held each week in central Bristol.

The City of Bristol Pipes and Drums

Bristol’s Pipe Band

The band has a diverse makeup of members, from all walks of life. including students, seasoned pensioners, respected banking officials, a passionate travel agent, influential figures from the business world, and even a skilled winemaker. Together, we form an eclectic ensemble that brings a dynamic energy to our performances.

Now under the expert leadership of Pipe Major Julian Smart, the City of Bristol Pipes & Drums has become established as a formidable force. With a core of 20 experienced players and a group of learners, our regular practice sessions always attract interest.

Whether the band takes the stage for a static concert, or marching in formation on parade, the City of Bristol Pipes & Drums proudly represents the spirit of the South West of England’s pipe band fraternity and the city of Bristol.

Bookings

Book the band for parades, festivals and private events.

The whole band can be hired for festivals, carnivals or sporting events. We have a busy program, with bookings often made up to a year in advance.

You can also contact the band to hire individual pipers and drummers or a small band for weddings, birthdays, anniversaries or funerals.
