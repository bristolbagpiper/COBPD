About the band

A Bristol band with a serious approach to performance and a friendly, local core.

Established in 1999, City of Bristol Pipes and Drums has become a familiar presence at civic occasions and public events in and around the city. The band performs at major remembrance ceremonies, festivals, carnivals and private occasions while continuing to welcome new players into the fold.

Members are unpaid for performances. Booking fees support the practical costs that keep the band going, including instruments, uniforms, maintenance, rehearsal space and travel.

Frequently asked questions

A few practical answers.

Can we ask the band to play at our event?

Yes. The best first step is the booking page, which helps direct your enquiry to the right person.

How much does a booking cost?

Cost depends on the type of event, location, duration and whether you need the full band, a smaller ensemble or an individual player.

Where does the band rehearse?

The band rehearses weekly in central Bristol.

Can beginners join?

Yes, but progress takes commitment, regular practice and time. The band can help connect enthusiastic learners with tuition and advice.

Can an experienced drummer join the snare line straight away?

Not always. Pipe band drumming has its own technique and repertoire, so players may need time, tuition and a progression route before taking a regular place.
