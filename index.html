About

A long-standing Bristol band with a strong public presence.

Established in 1999, City of Bristol Pipes and Drums has become a familiar part of civic and community life in the city. The band performs at major remembrance ceremonies, festivals, carnivals and private occasions, while continuing to welcome both experienced players and committed learners.

Members are unpaid for performances. Fees from bookings support instruments, uniform, rehearsal space, maintenance and travel, keeping the band active and performance-ready.

Join the band

Experienced players are welcome, and beginners can work toward the line-up.

The band welcomes pipers and drummers from Bristol and the wider South West. Experienced players may be able to join rehearsals quickly, while beginners can build toward performance through regular practice, support and tuition.

Open joining page
Can beginners join?

Yes, but it takes time, regular practice and commitment. The band can help point new players toward the right next steps and tuition.

Where does the band rehearse?

The band rehearses weekly in central Bristol.

Can experienced drummers join straight away?

Sometimes, but pipe band technique and repertoire are specific, so some players may need time before taking a regular place in the performing set.
