About the band

Public performances with the discipline of a parade band and the feel of a local one.

City of Bristol Pipes and Drums has been part of the city’s public life since 1999. The band performs at remembrance events, civic occasions, community festivals and private ceremonial events, while keeping a regular rehearsal and training pattern through the year.

Members come from Bristol and the wider South West. Some arrive with years of pipe band experience, others work steadily toward their first public performance. The band is volunteer-run, and appearances are funded in a practical way rather than treated like a commercial showpiece.

Bookings

For ceremonies, parades, festivals and private occasions.

The band can discuss a full parade presence, a smaller group or individual players, depending on the event. Typical enquiries include remembrance services, civic ceremonies, carnivals, weddings, anniversaries, funerals and memorials.

When getting in touch, include the date, location, approximate timing and whether you need the full band or a smaller line-up. Fees go back into the running costs of the band rather than individual performers.

Join the band

Experienced players and serious beginners are both welcome.

If you already play pipes or drums, you may be able to move into rehearsals quickly. If you are starting from scratch, the route is longer, but the band can help point you toward tuition and the right next step.

Pipe band playing takes regular practice and patience, especially for snare drumming and public marching repertoire. New members generally build toward the performing set over time rather than dropping straight into the line-up.

FAQ

Practical questions, answered plainly.

Can the band play at our event?

Usually, yes. Start with an email including the event type, date, location and the sort of line-up you have in mind.

How much does a booking cost?

Cost depends on the type of event, travel, timing, duration and the size of the line-up required.

Where does the band rehearse?

The band rehearses weekly in central Bristol.

Can beginners join?

Yes, but progress takes commitment. Beginners usually need regular personal practice and, in some cases, tuition before joining the public set.

Can an experienced drummer join the snare line straight away?

Not always. Pipe band drumming has its own technique and repertoire, so some players need time before taking a regular place in the performing line-up.
